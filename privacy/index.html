Hoardfire Privacy Policy

For the Android game Hoardfire: Idle Dragon Clicker. Last updated November 16, 2026.

Who we are

Hoardfire is made by Josip Papež, an independent developer. For any question about this policy or your data, email papezjosip@gmail.com.

The short version

Hoardfire has no accounts, no sign-in and no server of its own, and your progress stays on your phone. The game offers optional rewarded ads through Google AdMob. When ads are requested, Google collects some device data, described under Ads below.

What stays on your device

  • Game progress (gold, dragons, upgrades, settings) is saved only in the app's private storage on your device. It is never sent to us or anyone else.
  • Save codes. If you tap Share save code, the game turns your progress into a code and opens your phone's share menu. The code goes only where you choose to send it.
  • Reminders. If you turn them on, the game schedules local notifications on your phone: a reminder when your dragons stop earning while you are away and a note when a dragon finishes training. They are created on the device, no server is involved, and you can turn them off in the game's Settings or in Android's notification settings at any time.

Ads (Google AdMob)

Some boosts can be earned by choosing to watch a short ad. These ads come from Google AdMob. The Google Mobile Ads SDK in the game collects and shares the following with Google, for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention:

  • your device's IP address, which may be used to estimate its general location;
  • interactions such as app launches, taps and ad views;
  • diagnostic information such as app launch time and performance;
  • device identifiers: the Android advertising ID and the app set ID.

This data is encrypted in transit. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the game shows Google's consent message before any ads are requested, and you can change your choices at any time under Stats, Settings, Ad privacy choices. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. Google's use of this data is explained in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Permissions

  • Notifications, only if you allow them, for the reminders above.
  • Run at startup, so scheduled reminders survive a phone restart.
  • Vibration, for haptic feedback when you tap.
  • Internet access and advertising ID, used only by Google AdMob to request and show ads.

The game does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos, files or any account information.

Third parties

Hoardfire is distributed through Google Play. Google may collect information about your download and use of Google Play under Google's Privacy Policy. Apart from the ad data described above, which goes to Google AdMob, the game sends no data to Google or any other company.

Children

Hoardfire is meant for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect any data from anyone.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your game progress never leaves your device unless you share a save code, so you control it fully. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes it. We hold no copy, so there is nothing for us to delete. Data collected for ads is held by Google under its own policies.

Security

Your progress is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. The only network traffic is ad requests to Google, which are encrypted.

Changes to this policy

If the game adds features that handle data, such as purchases, this policy will be updated before they go live, and the date above will change.
