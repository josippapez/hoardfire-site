Hoardfire Privacy Policy

For the Android game Hoardfire: Idle Dragon Clicker. Last updated November 15, 2026.

Who we are

Hoardfire is made by Josip Papež, an independent developer. For any question about this policy or your data, email papezjosip@gmail.com.

The short version

Hoardfire does not collect, store on a server, sell or share any personal data. The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics and no advertising SDK. Your progress stays on your phone.

What stays on your device

  • Game progress (gold, dragons, upgrades, settings) is saved only in the app's private storage on your device. It is never sent to us or anyone else.
  • Reminders. If you turn them on, the game schedules local notifications on your phone: a reminder after you have been away for 12 hours and a note when a dragon finishes training. They are created on the device, no server is involved, and you can turn them off in the game's Settings or in Android's notification settings at any time.

Permissions

  • Notifications, only if you allow them, for the reminders above.
  • Run at startup, so scheduled reminders survive a phone restart.
  • Vibration, for haptic feedback when you tap.

The game does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos, files or any account information.

Third parties

Hoardfire is distributed through Google Play. Google may collect information about your download and use of Google Play under Google's Privacy Policy. The game itself sends no data to Google or any other company.

Children

Hoardfire is meant for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect any data from anyone.

Keeping and deleting your data

Because your data never leaves your device, you control it fully. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes all of it. We hold no copy, so there is nothing for us to delete.

Security

Your progress is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. Nothing is transmitted over the network.

Changes to this policy

If the game ever adds features that handle data, such as ads or purchases, this policy will be updated before they go live, and the date above will change.
