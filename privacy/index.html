Hoardfire Privacy Policy

For the Android game Hoardfire: Idle Dragon Clicker. Last updated November 17, 2026.

Who we are

Hoardfire is made by Josip Papež, an independent developer. For any question about this policy or your data, email papezjosip@gmail.com.

The short version

Hoardfire has no accounts and no server of its own, and your progress is saved on your phone. You can choose to sign in with Google Play Games: Google then keeps your achievements and a copy of your progress so you can continue on another phone, described under Google Play Games below. The game also offers optional rewarded ads through Google AdMob. When ads are requested, Google collects some device data, described under Ads below.

What stays on your device

  • Game progress (gold, dragons, upgrades, settings) is saved in the app's private storage on your device. It is never sent to us. If you sign in with Google Play Games, a copy is also saved to your Google Play Games account.
  • Save codes. If you tap Share save code, the game turns your progress into a code and opens your phone's share menu. The code goes only where you choose to send it.
  • Reminders. If you turn them on, the game schedules local notifications on your phone: a reminder when your dragons stop earning while you are away and a note when a dragon finishes training. They are created on the device, no server is involved, and you can turn them off in the game's Settings or in Android's notification settings at any time.

Google Play Games (optional)

If you sign in with Google Play Games, the game uses Google Play Games Services, and Google collects and stores:

  • your Play Games gamer identity (gamer name and avatar), which the game can see;
  • the achievements you unlock in the game;
  • a cloud copy of your game progress (Saved Games), with your play time and a cover picture, so you can continue on another phone;
  • analytics and diagnostic data that Google uses to keep Play Games Services stable and improve it.

This data is encrypted in transit and is held by Google under Google's Privacy Policy. Who can see your Play Games profile and achievements is set in your Play Games profile. If you don't sign in, none of this is sent. You can delete your Play Games data for this game, or your whole Play Games profile, in the Google Play Games app or your Google Account.

Ads (Google AdMob)

Some boosts can be earned by choosing to watch a short ad. These ads come from Google AdMob. The Google Mobile Ads SDK in the game collects and shares the following with Google, for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention:

  • your device's IP address, which may be used to estimate its general location;
  • interactions such as app launches, taps and ad views;
  • diagnostic information such as app launch time and performance;
  • device identifiers: the Android advertising ID and the app set ID.

This data is encrypted in transit. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the game shows Google's consent message before any ads are requested, and you can change your choices at any time under Stats, Settings, Ad privacy choices. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. Google's use of this data is explained in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Permissions

  • Notifications, only if you allow them, for the reminders above.
  • Run at startup, so scheduled reminders survive a phone restart.
  • Vibration, for haptic feedback when you tap.
  • Internet access, used by Google AdMob to request and show ads, and by Google Play Games if you sign in.
  • Advertising ID, used only by Google AdMob.

The game does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or files. The only account information it sees is your Play Games gamer identity, and only if you sign in.

Third parties

Hoardfire is distributed through Google Play. Google may collect information about your download and use of Google Play under Google's Privacy Policy. Apart from the ad data described above, which goes to Google AdMob, and the Google Play Games data described above if you sign in, the game sends no data to Google or any other company.

Children

Hoardfire is meant for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect any data from anyone.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your game progress stays on your device unless you share a save code or sign in with Google Play Games. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes the copy on your phone. A Google Play Games cloud copy can be deleted in the Google Play Games app or your Google Account. We hold no copy, so there is nothing for us to delete. Data collected for ads and for Play Games is held by Google under its own policies.

Security

Your progress is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. The only network traffic is ad requests to Google and, if you sign in, Google Play Games, both encrypted.

Changes to this policy

If the game adds features that handle data, such as purchases, this policy will be updated before they go live, and the date above will change.
